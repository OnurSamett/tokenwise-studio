How it works

One orchestrator. A whole team of models.

Grepping a repo doesn't need your strongest model. Debugging a race condition does. tokenwise makes that call for every piece of the job.

01

Describe the task

Type what you want in plain words. The orchestrator (Claude) reads the project and splits the work.

02

Right model, right effort

Each piece goes to the cheapest worker that can do it well, in its own git worktree.

search · summariesHaiku · low
routine codeCodex Sol · medium
hard bugs · designOpus · high
03

Review, then apply

Nothing touches your project until you approve it. Per-file diffs, one click to apply, one to commit.

Features

Built to stretch every plan.

⇄

Automatic fallback

A plan near its limit or a rate-limit error moves the job to the next worker: Codex, Gemini, Claude, then free models.

◎

Model and effort, separately

Opus at low for a quick judgment call, Sonnet at high for a big routine refactor. Effort is chosen per piece, not per session.

$0

Free models as real workers

Free NVIDIA models (GLM, Step, DeepSeek…) read and edit files in a sandboxed worktree, ranked by Terminal-Bench. Opt-in.

✓

You stay in control

The orchestrator can't edit your files. Every change comes from a worker you review before it lands.

▤

Live plan limits

See your Claude and ChatGPT 5-hour and weekly usage in the sidebar, read from the CLIs themselves.

⚙

Pick your team

Automatic mode, or choose who writes code, who reviews and who draws images, with Best quality, Balanced and Cheapest presets.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. tokenwise and tokenwise studio are open source (MIT). They run on your machine and use the plans and CLIs you already have; there is no tokenwise account or server.

Does it save usage, really?

On small tasks the overhead is about even; the savings come from long sessions with lots of reading, where mechanical work goes to cheaper models and other plans. We publish our benchmark harness so you can measure it on your own work.

Will my code be sent to free providers?

Only if you opt in. Free models are off until you add a key and set TOKENWISE_ALLOW_FREE=1, because free tiers may use prompts for training. They also never see secrets like .env files or keys.

Can a worker break my project?

Workers that edit files work in their own git worktree. Nothing reaches your project until you review the diff and press Apply, and applied changes are staged, not committed.

What do I need?

Node 20+, git, and at least one of Claude Code, the Codex CLI or Google Antigravity, signed in. macOS is the most tested platform.
